Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

[Pegasusx] (the “Operator”) values the privacy of users of “Moving Sketchbook” (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in the App.

1. Collection of Personal Information

The Operator does not collect or store the following personal information during use of the App.

  • Name, email address, telephone number, or physical address
  • Registration information or account information
  • Contacts or location information
  • Advertising identifiers or installation identifiers
  • App usage records or behavioral analytics information
  • Content created in the App, such as drawings and coloring activity
  • Error or crash diagnostic information

The App does not provide registration, login, advertising, payment, or analytics features.

2. Storage of App Data

Drawings and activity data created in the App are stored only on the user’s device. The Operator does not transmit this data to a server or access it remotely.

The policy of the relevant operating system provider may apply to device backups or backup features provided by the operating system.

3. Collection of Error and Diagnostic Information

The released version of the App does not itself use Unity Cloud Diagnostics, advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any separate remote error-reporting service.

However, for apps distributed through Google Play, Google Play may collect crash and ANR-related information (ANR means that an app is not responding) from Android devices whose users have opted in to automatically share usage and diagnostics data. This information may be reported to developers through Android Vitals and is processed in accordance with Google Play’s policies. This is separate from data collected or transmitted directly by the App to the Operator’s own servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not require children protected under applicable laws in their respective countries to provide personal information, register, log in, or submit an email address.

The Operator does not intentionally collect children’s personal information. If an inquiry is needed, a parent or legal representative should contact the Operator.

5. Email Inquiries

If a user contacts the Operator by email, the following information may be processed.

  • Purpose: To review and respond to the inquiry
  • Information processed: Email address, inquiry content, and information included by the user in the email
  • Retention period: Within three months after the inquiry has been handled
  • Deletion method: Irreversible deletion after the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been achieved

Users should not include unnecessary personal information or detailed personal information about children in inquiry emails.

6. Provision of Personal Information to and Entrustment to Third Parties

The Operator does not provide or entrust personal information collected during use of the App to third parties.

However, email inquiries may be transmitted and stored through the systems of an email service provider, and that provider’s privacy policy may apply.

7. Overseas Users and EU/EEA Users

The Operator does not collect personal information or track user activity during use of the App.

Users in the EU/EEA may request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of processing of their personal information, or object to its processing, by email. The Operator will review and handle such requests in accordance with applicable laws.
